USER PROFILE:

Marc Ribera is 34 years old and works as a recruitment manager at a software company in Girona.

He wants to quickly find out who the candidate is, what they have studied, where they have worked, and what skills they have, as well as being able to contact them without having to search for their details. He dislikes long, disorganized CVs that fail to clearly explain what the candidate has actually done.

That is why he needs a clear website that displays the most important information at a glance.


INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE:

For the layout of this CV, I have chosen a simple but effective design based on three main sections, arranged in a specific order to guide the reader.

The first section, and the one that attracts the most attention, is the blue box at the top. It contains my name and professional title, two essential pieces of information when considering a candidate for a job. That is why this section is highlighted in blue. It is the first thing the reader should see.

The second section is my profile, which is placed in a side column. It includes a photo of myself and other personal information, such as my contact details, my address and my language skills. Once the reader has seen who I am, this is where they can find out what I look like and how to reach me.

Finally, the main section has a white background and contains most of the detailed information. This includes a brief introduction about who I am, my education, my work experience and my personal skills. Recruiters will reach this section if I have already caught their interest as a potential candidate, which is the reasoning behind the order of the sections. From the most essential information to the most detailed.

VISUAL DESIGN

The design is simple, professional, and clean. Its main goal is to present the information clearly and ensure that it is well organized.

The colour palette I have chosen follows the same concept It is simple, as it consists of only four colours, but effective, as it highlights information according to its relevance and divides the content into three main sections The top, the sidebar, and the central area.

Regarding typography, I have used "Inter", which maintains consistency with the other sections. It is a simple typeface that serves its purpose effectively. I have also used different techniques to emphasize certain types of content, such as increasing the font size according to the importance of the information and highlighting key details in bold.

Finally, it is also important to highlight the use of space, as every design choice has a specific purpose. For example, most of the text is placed in the central area because it is the most comfortable space for reading longer texts.

It is also worth noting that the space between the end of a paragraph and the next heading is greater than the space between a heading and its corresponding text. This helps the reader visually distinguish between the five sections more easily.
